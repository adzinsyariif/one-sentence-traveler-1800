One sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to
a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from
things a person of that year could already picture: a road, letters, books,
voices, a horse. No list of features, just one picture they can hold in
their head.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes
the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The sentence

> The internet is a road joining house to house across the whole earth,
> carrying letters, books, and voices faster than any horse, at any hour
> of the day or night.
